SONYA'S STORY

We are asking for help for Sofya

Sofya (Sonya) Finkovskaya is at the centre of this family fundraising campaign. According to the information provided by her family, she is facing cancer, and her family is doing everything possible to support her through treatment, care and the difficult days ahead.

A serious illness can affect every part of family life. Medical care, travel, everyday needs and other expenses can become difficult for a family to manage at the same time. That is why the family is reaching out to people who may be able to help.

If you are able to donate, any amount can be meaningful to a family asking for support. If you cannot donate, sharing this page can help the appeal reach someone who may be able to contribute.

Thank you for stopping here, reading Sonya's story and considering helping her family.
