HER STORY

A childhood interrupted — and a life that continued.

Public accounts about Sofya Finkovskaya describe a serious illness that began when she was a child. Her treatment took her away from an ordinary school routine and placed enormous demands on Sofya and her family.

In 2015, a story about Sofya was featured by the Russian television project Day of Good Deeds. According to the published account, viewers responded to the family's situation and more than 17.9 million rubles were collected. The television page states that funds remaining after Sofya's treatment were directed to help other children through the charitable foundation involved in the campaign. Read the original 5-TV publication →

The story did not end with treatment. Years later, the charitable foundation Podari Zhizn published an interview with Sofya in 2021. In that interview, she described returning to school, preparing for university, staying close to her family, and remembering the people who supported her during treatment.

Owing to the people who stood beside her, a frightening chapter became a story of recovery, family, friendship and moving forward.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT

This page is an archive, not a new medical claim.

The public sources linked here are from earlier years. The 2021 foundation interview explicitly describes Sofya as a former beneficiary and says that she was doing well at that time.

For that reason, this website presents the material as a historical story and points readers to the original publications rather than presenting old medical information as evidence of a current condition.
